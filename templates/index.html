How to Play
  • Reach the Safe Zone: {{ SAFE_ZONE_DISTANCE }} progress, 10 stages.
  • Scavenge for items, Fight for XP and big progress, Rest to heal.
  • Click Use in the backpack: food gives rations, medicine heals, bullets give ammo.
  • Weapons and armor in your backpack make zombies hit softer.
  • Every {{ HORDE_EVERY }} days a horde attacks. More survivors = better defense.
  • At 100 XP you level up and pick a perk.
  • No food = you starve. Low morale = survivors desert you.
